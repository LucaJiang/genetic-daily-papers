## Scientific checks and unresolved source discrepancies

- Beyond exons: PDF pp. 3–4 specify π_i=1, S=0, 74 additive nonnegative annotation variance components, weighted marginal likelihood, and core/partial/full ACS. Polygenicity used for the cross-trait trend is a separate estimate. ACS is not h² fraction, a conditional annotation increment, or PIP. PDF p. 5 gives the percentage-point slopes and confidence intervals; p. 8 gives Figure 3. Noninfinitesimal sensitivity fits retain the h² profile. Claims relying on missing Figure S5 and other supplements are explicitly attributed to main-text reports.
- CASTIE: pp. 13–15 specify PQL/AI-REML, two random effects, SMW, score variance ratios, SPA and adaptive donor-cluster HC1 with threshold 1+2√(2/D). Actual input is SCTransform-corrected Poisson-scale counts plus depth offset. The tested cis variants have MAF>5% (p. 18, line 679). The 270 result is gene×trait×cell-compartment combinations, not genomic loci; 197=106+91. Ordinary genotype LD need not represent G×C statistic covariance. Figure 4g/text gives ATP1A3 H4=0.97 while its caption gives 0.9; this discrepancy is preserved in the web caption. The p. 15 repeated term in the cell-effect equation is resolved using the p. 14 full model. The reported 500-fold CPU summary cannot be reconstructed simply by dividing the median wall times and multiplying by the stated cores; benchmark tables are missing, and notes label these as author-reported summaries. The monocyte 247/1081 ratio is 22.8%, differing from the text's 21%; notes avoid that percentage. Cross-cell-group de-duplication behind 3,155/2,022 needs missing Tables 6–7.
- sc-pcQTL: pp. 8–10 distinguish hurdle co-expression screening from SAIGE-QTL association of cluster PCs. Both direction minima and component union are selection steps; a 0.0220 union rejection rate at α=.01 is not calibrated at .01. The main 2,040 PC count uses within-PC SNP BH, not global PC FDR. Fine-mapping uses donor LD, N=982, L=10, 95% credible sets with minimum |r|=.5; coloc.susie uses H4>.75. The 46 additional groups are connected signal groups, not independent disease loci. Figure 4 includes no-credible-set items that were not tested for colocalization; absence of a test is not evidence of absence. Missing supplement details on PIP weighting, SMR/HEIDI and dependent-cell simulations are not invented.
- AlphaGenome: 144,139 total aSNPs comprise 52,705 Denisovan, 40,583 Neanderthal 1KG, and 50,851 Neanderthal PNG; 67,170 are polymorphic in the 11 LCL donors. The three category counts do not partition 67,170. Input is a single-variant sequence contrast, not a joint haplotype input. Signed empirical q is not a p-value or PIP. Figure 1E shows Spearman correlation, not the caption's concordance; Figure 1F begins with TSS-distance groups; Figure 2A shows correlation forest plots, not the caption's absolute-quantile distribution. Web captions follow the visible figures and result text and disclose these discrepancies. MPRA comparisons use a much smaller active/comparable set than the original 17,078 assayed variants. The source active/inactive counts add to 17,077; no new denominator is inferred. Strand/duplicate-track aggregation, overlap-gene pairing and complete biosample lists remain unaudited. JAK1/TAB2 are nearby candidates, not experimentally proven target genes.
