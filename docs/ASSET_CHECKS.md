# Original figure verification, 2026-09-05

Six original publisher PNGs were downloaded through the review workflow and visually checked against the article text and captions. Figures are reproduced unchanged, with attribution and licence links. The site is a noncommercial academic reading site. No cropping, recoloring, synthetic reconstruction or alteration is applied to the original image bytes.

- Perée et al., DOI 10.1038/s41467-026-76672-4: Figure 5 (IBD risk-region matching), Figure 4 (blood/gut regulatory comparisons). CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, checked in the accepted manuscript.
- Jones et al., DOI 10.1038/s41586-026-10987-6: Figure 4 (SATB2 and allele-specific enhancer assay), Figure 3 (LHX2 perturbation). CC BY 4.0, checked in the publisher article.
- Hu et al., DOI 10.1038/s41467-026-77128-5: Figure 3 (test-set prediction), Figure 7 (risk stratification). CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, checked in the accepted manuscript.

Exact publisher URLs and SHA-256 checksums are retained in each paper's frontmatter. Build checks compare the downloaded or local image bytes with those visually verified checksums. The actual browser test additionally checks naturalWidth and successful rendering. The resource MAEEA remains abstract-only and has no unverified figure.
